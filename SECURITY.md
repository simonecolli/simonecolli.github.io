# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email security@simonecolli.com. Write in Italian or English.
Do not open a public issue for a security problem.

I reply within 7 days. When the problem is fixed, I tell you,
and I credit you if you want.

## Scope

- The site at https://www.simonecolli.com
- The contact form worker at `/api/` (`worker/`)

Out of scope: third-party services (GitHub Pages, Cloudflare,
Google Analytics), and reports from automated scanners
without a proof of impact.

## Supported versions

Only the version live on the site gets fixes.
